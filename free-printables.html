Printing tips

  • Print at 100% or "actual size" for the sharpest result. "Fit to page" also works fine.
  • The cards are set up for A4 and print just as well on US Letter. On Letter, choose "fit to page" and you will get a slightly wider white margin.
  • Want a smaller card? Choose A5 and everything scales down together, giving you a neat half-size version.
  • Ordinary printer paper is fine. Thicker card stock holds up better if they are going somewhere busy.
